## 🚀 Overview
The Payvibes Management System is an advanced, tech-driven platform designed to automate and optimize the entire supply chain and retail distribution network. By replacing traditional, manual record-keeping with real-time tracking and intelligent automation, this system bridges the gap between warehouses, delivery agents, and retail shops—ensuring products reach the right shelves at the exact right time with zero blind spots.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 📦 Live Inventory & Warehouse Control
* **Real-Time Stock Monitoring:** Instantly track stock levels across multiple warehouses, godowns, and distribution points from a single dashboard.
* **Batch & Expiry Management:** Accurately track products by batch numbers and expiry dates to eliminate dead stock and reduce financial losses.
* **Smart Replenishment Alerts:** Automated low-stock alerts and threshold notifications prevent unexpected stockouts.

### 🚚 Order-to-Delivery & Route Optimization
* **Seamless Order Processing:** Convert sales orders into digital invoices instantly with built-in stock validation.
* **Optimized Route Planning:** Integrated delivery tracking and route mapping help field teams save fuel and time.
* **Digital Proof of Delivery:** Record precise logs of what was delivered to each shop for total transparency.

### 💰 Customer & Financial Ledger Management
* **Multi-Shop Management:** Easily handle hundreds of retail customers under one unified system.
* **Automated Payment Tracking:** Keep a clear, running ledger for every customer; pending balances and credits are never lost.
* **Instant Notifications:** Automated payment reminders and order confirmations sent directly via SMS or WhatsApp.

### 📊 Advanced Analytics & Reporting
* **Profitability Insights:** Identify which retail routes, products, or customers drive the highest revenue.
* **Comprehensive Dashboards:** Access 50+ customizable reports covering sales velocity, cash flow, and staff performance.

## 💻 Tech Stack & Architecture
* **Frontend:** Responsive web interface and mobile-friendly layouts for field agents.
* **Backend:** Secure, scalable cloud architecture ensuring high availability.
* **Database:** Centralized cloud database with offline sync capabilities.
